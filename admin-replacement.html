Before you create a replacement memo
  • A replacement memo is a record of items being replaced for the same order. The original order keeps its status, its quantities and its totals — nothing on it changes.
  • There are no prices on a replacement memo, on this page or on the PDF.
  • It cannot be edited or deleted afterwards. If something is wrong, void it and create a new one.
