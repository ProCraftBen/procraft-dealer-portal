Before you create a return memo
  • A return memo is an internal record. The original order keeps its status, its quantities and its totals — nothing on it changes.
  • Item prices here do not include modification fees or assembly fees.
  • Any discount applied on the original order is already taken into account — the credit shown is the discounted price the dealer actually paid.
