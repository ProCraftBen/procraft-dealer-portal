How to create your first quote and order

Thanks for using the ProCraft DC Dealer Portal. Here you can build your own quote and submit an order once everything looks right. The whole process is about four steps — this page walks you through them. Ready? Let's go!

01

Step 1: Start a new estimate

Start from the dashboard by clicking New Estimate. This page covers the basics for the order — what the project is called, how it ships, where it goes, and who gets billed.

  • Enter a Job Name so you can find this order later, and put anything we should know in Notes (Optional)
  • Choose your Logistic method
  • Confirm the Shipping / Delivery Address
  • Confirm the Billing Address

Once everything looks right, click Next: Select Products → to continue.

Video: starting a new estimate and filling in the order information
02

Step 2: Choose door styles and SKUs

This page is where you pick your door style and cabinets. Every price you see already has your own multiplier applied — there's nothing for you to calculate.

  • Pick a style from Door Styles at the top
  • Search for the SKU Code you need below and enter a quantity
  • Click Assembled or Unassembled — the item is added to your cart automatically
  • Repeat until everything is in. You can open Quote Items at any time to check
  • When it looks right, click Continue →
Video: choosing a door style, adding cabinets, and checking selected items
03

Step 3: Set up modifications

This page is where you adjust cabinet details — hinge direction, depth, glass doors and so on. If the order needs no adjustments at all, click Review → to skip it.

  • Click any Assembled item to open its setup window
  • The modifications available for that item are listed in the window — set them as needed
  • Click Save when you're done to close the window
Anything not on the list that we should know about can go in Modification Note.
If an item has no options to set, the window will show only Modification Note.

Once every item is handled, click Review → for the final step.

Video: opening the setup window, configuring a modification, and saving
Same SKU, but each one needs different modifications?

In the setup window, use Split this SKU to divide the quantity into groups and set each group separately. For example, three of the same B30 can be set to left, right, and both. After you save, the Review → page lists the three groups separately.

Video: using Split this SKU to divide one item into three separate groups
Want to change the door style on the whole order?

On the modification page, click Swap Style to change them all at once. The system shows the price difference first; it only takes effect after you click Confirm Swap and then Confirm Change. The Modifications Total: at the bottom right updates at the same time.

Note: you can only swap within the same construction type. Framed can only be swapped for Framed, and Frameless for Frameless.
Video: using Swap Style to change the door style and confirm the price change
04

Step 4: Review and submit

The last page shows the complete order — order information, delivery address, every item and every amount. Please read it through before submitting. Once it looks right, you have two options:

Save Draft
Saves it without submitting. You can find it again under My Orders and keep editing. Good for when you're still working things out with your customer.
Submit Purchase Order
Submits the order for real. A PO number is generated, and the order can no longer be edited by you.

After you submit, our team takes over. You can check progress any time under My Orders.

Video: reviewing the order and submitting the purchase order
05

What happens after you submit

Once your purchase order is in, our team handles it from there. It generally goes through these stages:

  • Stock confirmation — we check that everything you ordered can be supplied
  • Payment opens — once confirmed, you can complete payment on the order page
  • Order processing — after payment clears, we begin preparation and production
  • Delivery complete — the order ships and is closed out

Understanding order statuses

Every order shows its current status under My Orders. Below is what each one means and whether it needs anything from you.

Draft → Stock Review → Waiting for Payment → Payment Processing → Order Processing → Order Completed
Revising

Revising is not a stage in the flow — it means the ball is back in your court. It can appear after Stock Review or after Waiting for Payment. The order is flagged ⚠ Action Required and moves to the top of My Orders. When you see it, we're waiting on you; once you handle it, the order rejoins the normal flow.

Draft
Means
The order is saved but not submitted. Only you can see it.
You do
Come back and edit it any time. When you're ready, open it and click Submit Purchase Order.
Stock Review
Means
You've submitted, and we're confirming stock and availability for your items.
You do
Nothing needed from you at this stage — just wait for us. If anything needs adjusting, we'll send the order back to you.
Waiting for Payment
Means
Stock is confirmed and the order is ready for payment.
You do
Open the order, click Pay Now, and choose a payment method to complete it.
Revising
Means
We've looked at the order and something needs your confirmation or a change.
You do
Open the order and read the note we left, click Review & Resubmit, make your changes, then submit again with Request Review & Resubmit on the final page.
Payment Processing
Means
We've received your payment instruction and are confirming the funds.
You do
Nothing needed. You can no longer change the payment method yourself at this point — contact us if you need to switch.
Order Processing
Means
Payment is confirmed and the order has moved into preparation and production.
You do
Nothing needed — wait for our shipping notice.
Order Completed
Means
The order has been delivered, and a Fulfillment Date is shown on it.
You do
This order is finished. To order again, start a new estimate.

Two ending statuses

Closed — the order is closed out and will not move forward.

Cancelled — the order was cancelled. If you're not sure why, please contact us.
